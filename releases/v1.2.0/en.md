Prices and price-drop alerts

### Features
- **prices**: a new Prices page with your alerts, the games you follow, your to-play list at today's prices and the deals of the moment
- **prices**: every game page shows the best price now and where, the other shops, the lowest ever and how the price moved
- **prices**: follow a game's price on any sale or below a price you choose, and get one alert per drop, with a count on the Prices tab
- **prices**: prices come from IsThereAnyDeal with a free key (Admin → Prices: dozens of shops in your currency), or from CheapShark without one (PC shops, US dollars)
- **android**: price alerts as phone notifications; turn them on in the Prices page, and tapping one opens the game
