Assistant history and shelf changes from the chat

### Features
- **assistant**: ask it to add a game, mark it finished, rate it, log hours or remove it — it proposes the change in a card and nothing changes until you confirm
- **assistant**: conversations are kept on the server and listed beside the chat, to reopen later
- **assistant**: answers keep being written when you leave the page; coming back shows them still going, or finished
- **assistant**: select one or several old conversations and delete them together
