# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text is at that link; in short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people using the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
representing the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/TTiagoSousa). Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, and warn, suspend or ban
anyone whose behaviour goes against this code, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
